<objective>
Implement matching.py — the cross-document field comparison module that consumes two ResultadoAnalise objects (ART + Atestado) and produces a ResultadoCruzamento with per-field MATCH/DIVERGENTE/AUSENTE status. Purpose: Core matching logic that enables the pipeline to detect divergences between ART and Atestado documents, including the critical Par 2 company name divergence.
Output: Working matching.py with full unit test coverage (mocked GPT), conftest fixture.
